How to use Ava

From a blank field to an embed in a few steps.

  1. 01

    Sign up

    Create an account from this page. We’ll email a verification button — the account is created only after you click it.

  2. 02

    Shape the field

    In Studio, pick a letter, type a word, or upload an image. Density, merge, colors, and glyph weight control how the big form appears.

  3. 03

    Animate every glyph

    Turn on Wave, Bubble, Pulse, Ripple, and more. Each effect has its own sliders and always hits the whole field — not just the outline.

  4. 04

    Save, embed, download

    Save to your library. Copy an embed iframe, or download 4K / 1080p / 720p MP4 or a WebM. Delete a save and its embeds go offline.

Inside the studio

Everything you need for kinetic type.

Letter, word, image

Form a single letter, a short word (each glyph uses its own lowercase), or a photo built from random letters.

Dedicated animation controls

Wave, drift, shake, pointer spin, pulse, twinkle, ripple, and a spherical bubble lens — each with its own amount, speed, and scale.

Library + embed

Saves live in your library. Embeds stay live only while that save exists. Video export at 4K, 1080p, 720p, or web.
